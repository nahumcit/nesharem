This is the first post on **nesharem**, my corner of the internet. There is no single topic: drawing, football, philosophy, theology, economics and the things I play, watch and read will all live here.

The idea is simple: a place of my own, without noise or algorithms, where I can write calmly and keep track of what interests me. It is also an excuse to draw more and to finish the books I keep putting off.

## What you will find

- **Gallery**: my drawings, uploaded by hand.
- **Anime, games and books**: lists with covers, status and score.
- **Blog**: longer posts with topics, archives by year and month, and author.
- **Ko-fi**: my latest works, straight from there.

If something fails to load or a cover is missing, it can be filled in manually from the editor. No databases or servers: everything is static and free.

Thanks for stopping by.
